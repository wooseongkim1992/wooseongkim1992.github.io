Wooseong Kim is a postdoctoral researcher at the Unit of Occupational Medicine, Institute of Environmental Medicine, [Karolinska Institutet](https://ki.se) (Sweden), where he works in the *New World of Work* research group led by Theo Bodin. He is also affiliated with the Stockholm University Demography Unit (SUDA).

His research sits at the intersection of social epidemiology, sociology, and demography. He studies how labour market conditions, such as precarious employment, in-work poverty, and overqualification, shape mental health and family life, and how immigrants and their children integrate into the Swedish labour market. His work draws primarily on Swedish population register data.

His research has been published in journals such as *Social Science & Medicine* and *Research in Social Stratification and Mobility*. He serves as Associate Editor of the *Nordic Journal of Migration Research*.

He received his PhD in Social Demography from Stockholm University, with a dissertation on overqualification among workers from abroad, and holds a master's degree from Lund University.
